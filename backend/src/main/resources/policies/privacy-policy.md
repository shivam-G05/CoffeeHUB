This Privacy Policy explains what personal information CoffeeHub Global collects, how we use it and the choices you have. It applies to coffeehubglobal.com and our related services.

## Information we collect

**Account information:** your name, email address, mobile number and password (stored only in encrypted, hashed form). Buyers may optionally add a company name and GST number.

**Orders and addresses:** delivery and billing addresses, the items you buy, order notes and payment method. We do not store card or UPI credentials.

**Seller information:** business name, contact person, business type, address, website, verification documents (such as GST, PAN, FSSAI, company registration, IEC and address proof) and bank account details for payouts.

**Communications:** messages you send through the platform, requirements you post, quotations, reviews, disputes and any evidence you upload.

**Security and usage data:** sign-in history (including IP address, browser type and whether the attempt succeeded), and on-site activity such as searches, product views and checkout steps.

## How we use it

- To create and secure your account, including email verification and password reset.
- To process orders, pass delivery details to the seller fulfilling them, and show order status and tracking.
- To verify sellers and keep unsafe or fraudulent businesses off the marketplace.
- To route requirements to relevant suppliers and let you compare quotations.
- To send you notifications about orders, requirements, quotations, verification and disputes.
- To investigate disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.
- To understand how the marketplace is used and improve it.

## Who we share it with

- **Sellers you buy from** receive the details needed to fulfil your order, such as your name, phone number and delivery address.
- **Buyers** see a seller's business name, location, verification status and ratings. Your personal contact details are not shown to other users by default.
- **Service providers** who host our website and database, deliver email, and supply fonts, acting on our instructions.
- **Authorities** where required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

We do not sell your personal information.

## How long we keep it

We keep account information while your account is active, and order, invoice and tax records for as long as the law requires. Security logs are kept for a limited period to protect accounts.

## Security

Passwords are hashed, access to account areas is controlled by role, and our team accesses personal data only when needed to operate the marketplace or resolve an issue.

## Your rights

You can view and update your profile and addresses from your account. To request a copy of your data, correction or deletion of your account, email support@coffeehub.in. Some records, such as completed orders and invoices, must be kept to meet legal obligations.

## Children

CoffeeHub Global is intended for adults and businesses, and is not directed at anyone under 18.

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
