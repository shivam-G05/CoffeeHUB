Products on CoffeeHub Global are shipped by the sellers who sell them. This policy explains what to expect.

## Who ships your order

Each seller packs and dispatches its own items using a courier of its choice. If your cart contains products from several sellers, your order is split into one shipment per seller (for example CH-100001-A and CH-100001-B), and each arrives separately.

## Delivery areas and charges

Sellers deliver within India. Each seller sets the shipping charge for its products; it is shown on the product page and included in your total at checkout before you place the order.

## Dispatch and delivery times

Each product page shows the seller's usual dispatch time. Delivery time after dispatch depends on the courier and your location. Bulk orders, green coffee lots, made-to-order equipment and private-label work can take longer; for requirements you post, the seller's lead time is stated in their quotation.

## Tracking your order

When a seller ships your order, they add the courier name, tracking number, tracking link and dispatch date. You can see these on your order page, and we notify you when the status changes.

## Receiving your order

Please check the package on delivery. If it is damaged, missing items or incorrect, take photos and raise a dispute from your order page. Once you have received everything, you can confirm delivery from the order page.

## Delays

If your order has not arrived within a reasonable time after dispatch, message the seller from your order page. If the issue is not resolved, raise a dispute under Buyer Protection.

## Questions

Email support@coffeehub.in with your order number.
