CoffeeHub Global is a marketplace for coffee and the equipment, accessories and supplies used to make, sell and package it. Sellers may list only products in our categories that they are legally allowed to sell. The following are not allowed.

## Not allowed

- Products outside the coffee trade, such as unrelated groceries, electronics or clothing.
- Food products without a valid FSSAI licence, or that are expired, spoiled, adulterated or mislabelled.
- Coffee or blends making false claims about origin, grade, estate, certification (for example organic or fair trade) or health benefits.
- Counterfeit, replica or unauthorised branded goods, or products that infringe trademarks, copyright or designs.
- Equipment that is stolen, unsafe, recalled, or that does not meet applicable Indian safety and electrical standards.
- Used or refurbished equipment that is not clearly described as such, with its condition stated accurately.
- Products containing cannabis, narcotics or any controlled substance, or supplements and "functional" coffees sold with medical claims.
- Alcohol, tobacco and nicotine products.
- Hazardous materials such as flammable gases or pressurised cylinders.
- Any product whose sale is restricted or banned under Indian law.

## Listing rules

- Listings must describe the actual product being sold, with accurate photos, quantities, pricing and specifications.
- Personal contact details, external payment links and off-platform offers are not allowed in listings.
- Sellers must hold every licence and registration needed to sell their products.

## Enforcement

We review all new listings before they go live and may reject, remove or suspend any listing that breaks these rules. Repeated or serious violations can lead to suspension of the seller's account and, where required, a report to the authorities.

## Report a listing

If you see a product that breaks these rules, email support@coffeehub.in with the product link.
