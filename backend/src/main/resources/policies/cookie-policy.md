This policy explains how CoffeeHub Global stores information in your browser.

## We do not use advertising or tracking cookies

The CoffeeHub Global website does not set advertising cookies, and we do not use third-party tracking cookies to follow you across other websites.

## What we store in your browser

We use your browser's local storage and session storage, which work similarly to cookies, for these purposes only:

- **Keeping you signed in** (local storage): a sign-in token and your basic profile, so you stay logged in between visits. Logging out removes them.
- **Usage measurement** (session storage): a random session identifier used to count visits, searches and steps such as starting a requirement or checkout, so we can improve the marketplace. It is deleted when you close the browser tab and is not used for advertising.
- **Saving your work** (session storage): a draft of a requirement you have started but not yet posted, so it is not lost if you sign in mid-way.

These items are strictly necessary for the features you use, or used only to measure and improve the service.

## Third-party content

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser downloads them, Google receives standard request information such as your IP address. Google's handling of this data is described in its own privacy policy.

## Your choices

You can clear local and session storage at any time from your browser settings. If you do, you will be signed out and any unsent requirement draft will be lost.
