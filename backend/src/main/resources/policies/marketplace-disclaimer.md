CoffeeHub Global is an online marketplace that connects buyers with independent coffee businesses. Please read this disclaimer before you buy.

## Sold and fulfilled by third-party sellers

Unless a product page clearly states otherwise, every product on CoffeeHub Global is listed, sold, priced and shipped by an independent seller, not by CoffeeHub Global. The seller's business name is shown on every product page, order and invoice. The contract of sale for a product is between you and that seller.

## What "verified" means

The "CoffeeHub Global Verified" badge means our team reviewed the seller's business documents, such as GST registration, PAN, bank and address proof, before allowing them to sell. It confirms that the business exists and is registered; it is not a guarantee of product quality or of the seller's future conduct.

## Product information

Product descriptions, specifications, origins, grades, certifications, photos, prices and stock levels are provided by sellers. We review listings before they go live, but we cannot independently test every product. If a listing is inaccurate, report it to us or raise a dispute under Buyer Protection.

## Quotations

Quotations sent in response to a requirement are offers from the quoting seller. CoffeeHub Global does not guarantee that any seller will quote, or the price, availability or lead time a seller offers.
