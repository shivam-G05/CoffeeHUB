CoffeeHub Global is a marketplace: the products you buy are sold and shipped by independent, verified sellers. Buyer Protection explains what we do to keep your purchase safe and what happens if something goes wrong.

## Verified sellers only

Before a business can list anything on CoffeeHub Global, our team reviews its documents, including GST registration, PAN, bank proof and address proof, plus an FSSAI licence for food products. Only approved businesses carry the "CoffeeHub Global Verified" badge, and every new product listing is reviewed before it goes live.

## Your order is covered when

- the item does not arrive;
- it arrives damaged;
- you receive the wrong item, or the wrong quantity;
- the item is materially different from its listing, or of unacceptable quality;
- the seller stops responding to you.

## How to get help

1. Contact the seller first from your order page. Many problems are solved fastest this way.
2. If that does not resolve it, raise a dispute from the order page. Choose the reason, describe the problem and attach photos or documents as evidence.
3. The seller is asked to respond. Our team then reviews the order history, the evidence and both sides' messages, and records a decision.
4. If the dispute is decided in your favour, we arrange a full or partial refund as set out in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Please raise problems promptly after delivery. Disputes raised long after delivery are harder to investigate.

## Keep your purchase on CoffeeHub Global

Buyer Protection only covers orders placed and paid through CoffeeHub Global, with conversations kept in on-platform messages. If a seller asks you to pay or arrange the deal outside the platform, please decline and tell us.

## When protection does not apply

- Orders cancelled or refunded before a dispute was raised.
- Problems caused after delivery by misuse, incorrect storage or normal wear.
- Changes of mind about a product that matches its listing, unless the seller agrees to take it back.
- Deals arranged off the platform.
