This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work on CoffeeHub Global. Because each seller fulfils its own part of an order, cancellations and refunds are handled per seller order (for example CH-100001-A), not only for the order as a whole.

## Cancelling an order

**You can cancel** a seller's part of your order from your order page until the seller starts processing it (while it is placed, payment-confirmed or accepted). Once the seller has begun preparing or shipping the item, it can no longer be cancelled from your account; if there is a problem, raise a dispute instead.

**A seller may cancel** an order before it is shipped, for example if the item is out of stock. You will be notified with the reason.

**CoffeeHub Global may cancel** an order where we suspect fraud, a pricing error or a breach of our Terms.

## Refunds

- If you cancel before paying (for example a Cash on Delivery order), nothing is charged and no refund is needed.
- If a paid order is cancelled, it is marked "refund requested" and our team issues the refund.
- If a dispute is decided in your favour, we issue a full or partial refund depending on the outcome.
- Refunds are made to the original payment method where possible, or by bank transfer to an account in your name. We aim to process approved refunds within 7 working days; your bank may take additional time to show the credit.

## Returns

CoffeeHub Global does not offer change-of-mind returns. Returns and refunds are available when an item arrives damaged, defective, spoiled, incorrect, short in quantity or not as described; raise a dispute from your order page and, if a return is needed, we will tell you how to send the item back. Coffee and other food products cannot be returned once opened, for hygiene and food-safety reasons, unless one of these problems applies. A seller may also agree to take back an item in other cases; please arrange this through on-platform messages so there is a record.

## Partial refunds

Where only part of an order is affected, such as one damaged item or a short quantity, we may refund that part only.

## Questions

Email support@coffeehub.in with your order number.
