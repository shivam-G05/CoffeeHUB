This policy explains how CoffeeHub Global handles problems with an order that the buyer and seller cannot solve between themselves.

## When you can raise a dispute

A buyer can raise a dispute on a seller order once it has moved past "placed", unless the order has already been cancelled or refunded. Only one dispute can be open on a seller order at a time.

## Reasons

- Order not received
- Arrived damaged
- Wrong item sent
- Quality issue
- Quantity issue
- Not as described in the listing
- Seller unresponsive
- Other

## How it works

1. **Buyer raises the dispute** from the order page, choosing a reason, describing the problem and attaching evidence such as photos, packaging, invoices or messages.
2. **Seller responds** with their side of the story and any evidence. Sellers are expected to respond promptly; a seller who does not respond may have the dispute decided against them.
3. **CoffeeHub Global reviews** the order history, tracking information, evidence and on-platform messages, and may ask either party for more information.
4. **We record a decision**: a full refund, a partial refund, or no refund. The order is updated and both parties are notified with the reason.

## Fair decisions

We decide disputes on the evidence available. Fraudulent disputes, altered evidence or attempts to move the deal off the platform can lead to account suspension for buyers and sellers alike.

## Effect on sellers

Refunds decided in a dispute are deducted from the amount payable to the seller. Repeated upheld disputes may lead to listing removal or suspension of the seller's account.
