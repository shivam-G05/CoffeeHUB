These Seller Terms apply to every business that sells on CoffeeHub Global, in addition to our Terms & Conditions.

## Eligibility and verification

To sell, you must be a registered business in India and complete verification. You must upload a GST certificate, PAN, bank proof (such as a cancelled cheque) and address proof, plus an FSSAI licence if you sell food products, and any other licence your products require. Our team reviews your documents before approval. Uploading documents does not by itself grant approval or the verified badge, and we may reject, suspend or withdraw approval if information is incomplete, inaccurate or no longer valid.

## Listings

- Every new or edited product is reviewed before it is published.
- Listings must be accurate and complete, including category-specific details such as origin, grade, process, roast level and pack size for coffee, and condition for equipment.
- Keep prices, stock and dispatch times up to date.
- Products must comply with our Prohibited Products policy and all applicable laws.

## Orders and fulfilment

- Accept or cancel new orders promptly, and only cancel before shipping for genuine reasons such as unavailability.
- Pack items securely and ship within your stated dispatch time.
- Add the courier name, tracking number, tracking link and dispatch date when you ship.
- You are responsible for the product, its packaging, its delivery and any invoice or tax documents you are required to issue.

## Requirements and quotations

When you quote on a buyer's requirement, your quotation (price per unit, MOQ, available quantity, GST, shipping, lead time, validity and sample cost) is a binding offer for its validity period if the buyer selects it.

## Fees and payouts

- A commission is charged on each sale at the rate set for the product's category; contact us for the current rates for your categories. Online payments may also carry a payment-gateway fee. Each order's commission, fees and payout are shown in the Payments section of your seller dashboard.
- Your payout for each order is the order value less commission, applicable fees and any refunds.
- Payouts are made to your verified bank account after delivery, according to our settlement schedule. Changes to bank details must be verified before further payouts.

## Disputes and refunds

You must respond to buyer disputes promptly through the platform. Refunds decided under our Dispute Policy are deducted from your payout.

## Conduct

- Keep all communication and payment for CoffeeHub Global orders on the platform.
- Do not share personal contact details to take buyers off the platform, manipulate reviews or misrepresent your business.
- Treat buyer information as confidential and use it only to fulfil their orders.

## Suspension

We may suspend listings or your account for breaches of these terms, upheld disputes, fraud, or legal or regulatory reasons. We will tell you the reason where we are allowed to.
